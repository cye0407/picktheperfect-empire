Best Potatoes for Potato Salad

Good potato salad requires potatoes that hold their shape when cut and mixed. Waxy varieties are designed for exactly this.

Published: 2026-01-23

What Actually Matters for Potato Salad

Potato salad failures typically come from wrong variety choice, not technique problems.

Cell structure

Waxy potatoes have tightly bound cells that resist breaking apart when boiled and mixed. Starchy potatoes have loose cells that crumble under the same conditions.

Starch content

Low-starch potatoes maintain structural integrity when cooled. High-starch potatoes absorb too much dressing and fall apart into mush.

Moisture level

Moist waxy potatoes stay cohesive. Dry starchy potatoes become mealy and disintegrate. The moisture acts as a binder for the potato's structure.

Skin characteristics

Thin-skinned varieties work best for potato salad. The skin stays tender when boiled and doesn't need to be peeled, saving time and waste.

Potato Types That Excel for Salad

Red potatoes

Red potatoes are the standard for potato salad. They hold shape perfectly, have creamy texture, and thin edible skins. They're reliable in any potato salad style.

Tradeoff: less distinct flavor than some specialty varieties. They're neutral rather than characterful.

New potatoes

Young potatoes of any variety have especially waxy texture and paper-thin skins. They're ideal for potato salad and require minimal prep.

Tradeoff: seasonal availability. New potatoes are only available for limited periods after harvest.

Fingerling potatoes

Fingerlings add visual interest and hold shape excellently. Their small size means they can be used whole or halved, creating distinctive presentation.

Tradeoff: higher price per pound. More expensive than standard salad potatoes.

Yellow waxy varieties

Some yellow potatoes are waxy rather than starchy. They combine good shape retention with buttery flavor, making richer-tasting salads.

Tradeoff: requires knowing specific varieties. Not all yellow potatoes are waxy — Yukon Golds are borderline.

Common Potato Salad Mistakes

Using russets or other starchy potatoes

Russets are optimized for baking and frying, not boiling and mixing. They absorb dressing excessively and break into pieces. No mixing technique prevents this.

Overcooking even correct varieties

Overcooked waxy potatoes still break down. The correct approach: boil just until fork-tender, then stop. But even slightly overcooked waxy potatoes perform better than perfectly cooked starchy ones.

Mixing hot potatoes with dressing

Hot potatoes absorb dressing and become soggy. Cool them first for salad with distinct potato pieces. This is technique, but it interacts with variety — waxy potatoes tolerate the mistake better.

Regional Preferences

German potato salad uses waxy varieties exclusively. American potato salad traditionally uses red or white waxy potatoes. Southern potato salad often uses russets for deliberately creamy texture.

These aren't arbitrary preferences. Each style selects varieties for specific structural outcomes.
